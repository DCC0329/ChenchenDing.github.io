--- layout: archive title: "News" permalink: /teaching/ author_profile: true --- # Upcoming Conferences & Presentations ## 🌎 **AGU Fall Meeting — New Orleans, December 2025** **Poster Presentation** **Title:** Seasonal wetlands mapping using CYGNSS and Sentinel-1 data **Authors:** Chenchen Ding, Hangkai You, Min Chen **Session:** Remote Sensing for Hydrology and Surface Water **Format:** Poster **Status:** Accepted This work integrates GNSS-R (CYGNSS) and SAR (Sentinel-1) observations to estimate water fractions and capture seasonal wetland dynamics across the Greater Sudan Region. The project includes daily time series generation, data fusion workflows, and high-performance geospatial processing.
