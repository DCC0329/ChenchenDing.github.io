Who I am

I'm Chenchen Ding, a Master’s student in the Environmental Observation & Informatics (EOI) program at the Nelson Institute, University of Wisconsin–Madison (Expected graduation: August 2026).

I am also a research intern at the Global Change Research Laboratory, where I work on large-scale remote sensing and environmental data analysis projects.

How I work

I enjoy working with:

  • large satellite raster datasets
  • HPC / cloud environments
  • tiling pipelines and efficient data engineering
  • deep learning workflows and model explainability (Permutation, SHAP)

Where I’m Headed

I aim to become a researcher and data scientist working on satellite-based environmental monitoring, with a focus on water, vegetation, and climate resilience. I am passionate about building scalable pipelines that make global environmental data more accessible.

Beyond Academia

Outside research, I enjoy running, hiking, photography, and exploring new parks and trails around Wisconsin. I also love food, cooking, and discovering new cafés.
