Who I am

I'm Chenchen(陈陈)! A Master’s student in the Environmental Observation & Informatics (EOI) program at the Nelson Institute, University of Wisconsin–Madison (Expected graduation: August 2026).

I am also a research intern at the Global Change Research Laboratory, where I work with satellite observations to analyze environmental change at large scales.

How I work

I enjoy working with:

  • Large satellite raster datasets
  • HPC / cloud environments
  • Deep learning workflows and model explainability

Where I’m Headed

I want to build a career at the intersection of remote sensing and environmental data science, using satellite observations to track changes in water, vegetation, and climate.
